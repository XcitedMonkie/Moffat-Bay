About Us

Moffat Bay Lodge is located on Joviedsa Island in Washington State. It's a great place for visitors to get away from their everyday routines, relax, and enjoy the scenery that the Pacific Northwest has to offer.

Moffat Bay Lodge is part of the Moffat Bay resort and marina project. The lodge gives visitors a place to stay while they enjoy the island and everything it has to offer. Whether you want to spend time outdoors, explore the area, or just relax for a few days, Moffat Bay Lodge has something for everyone.
